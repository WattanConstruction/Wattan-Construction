About Us

Built on heavy equipment and hands-on site experience.

Wattan Construction is a heavy machinery and construction company based in Islamabad, Pakistan. We deal in a wide range of construction machinery and provide services for construction sites.

Our business includes the import of heavy machinery and construction-site work. Through our experience working on major construction projects, we aim to provide dependable machinery and services to our clients.
